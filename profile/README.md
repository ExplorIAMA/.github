ExplorIA makes artificial intelligence workable. We turn the complexity of AI into a clear course: solid skills, useful solutions and products that actually run, measured on your data, inside your systems.

## Three ways in

- **Training: make your teams autonomous.** Three tracks (executives, developers and data teams, business teams), in short sessions, on site or remote, in French, English or Arabic, on your own material.
- **Custom: build what fits you.** Scoping, a POC on your real data, then a solution plugged into your systems: prediction, computer vision, NLP and LLMs, real time and data.
- **Products: move faster with our building blocks.** Packaged AI modules and plug-in SaaS, including **Cabestan**, our automation platform run on n8n and hosted in Morocco.

## ExplorIA Academy

Learn AI online, at your own pace. Twelve courses across four tracks (foundations, deep learning, generative AI, decision-makers), with games, simulations, memes and a podcast for every step. In English, French and Darija, no account or password needed.

→ [academy.exploria.ma](https://academy.exploria.ma)

## How we work

**Observe → Chart → Prove → Deploy.** Success criteria written before the code, a POC within weeks, and an honest verdict when AI is not the right answer.

- **Your data stays yours:** on premises, with a Moroccan host or in the cloud region you choose.
- **What we build belongs to you:** the code, the models and the documentation.
- **No ghost team:** every engagement is led directly by a co-founder, with no subcontracting.

## What we work on

Machine learning · Computer vision · NLP · LLMs · AI agents · Chatbots · RAG · Fine-tuning · Private LLMs · MLOps · Data engineering · Data science · Process automation · n8n · Predictive maintenance · Real-time industrial AI
